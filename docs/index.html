Pointing with a mouse cursor on a shared screen is slow and easy to miss. Chalkberry lets you mark the exact spot, so people see what you mean the first time.

How it works

  1. 1

    Download and open

    Run the installer, or the portable file if you don’t want to install anything. A small toolbar appears at the top of your screen.

  2. 2

    Hold Ctrl and drag

    Draw over any app, website or video. The cursor turns into a pen so you always know you’re drawing.

  3. 3

    Let go and keep working

    Your drawings stay while you click and type as usual. Press Ctrl + Alt + C to clear them.

Everything you need, nothing you don’t

Draw over anything

Smooth chalk-style lines on top of every window, on every monitor. Pick from 8 colours, 5 thicknesses and 4 opacities, so you can highlight without hiding what’s underneath.

Erase one line, or all of them

Made a mistake? Hold Ctrl and right-click a line to remove just that one, or right-drag across several. Clear everything with one click on the bin.

A toolbar that stays out of the way

Drag it anywhere, or hide it. Every button explains itself when you rest the mouse on it. The same options are in the tray icon next to the clock.

Screenshot your drawings

Click the camera to capture the screen with your notes on it. Drag a box to keep only part of it, then save it as a PNG to share in chat, email or a ticket.

  • Remembers your choices. Colour, thickness, toolbar spot and save folder are kept for next time.
  • Light on your computer. Uses no processor power while you’re not drawing.
  • No account, no internet. Nothing to sign up for. It runs entirely on your PC.
  • Easy to remove. Uninstall from Windows Settings like any other app.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for everyone, at home or at work. No trial, no ads, no account. It’s open source under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can read and check the code.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that aren’t signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. The source code is public on GitHub, and every release lists a checksum so you can check your download matches.

Installer or portable: which one should I pick?

The installer (ChalkberrySetup) adds Chalkberry to the Start menu and can start it with Windows. The portable file runs without installing: put it anywhere and double-click it.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

Yes. You can draw on all of them, and screenshots capture all of them.

Will it slow down my computer?

No. It’s a small native Windows app and does nothing while you aren’t drawing.

Will people on my call see the drawings?

Yes, when you share your whole screen. The drawings are on top of everything, so screen sharing picks them up.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No, Chalkberry is made for Windows 10 and 11 only.
