## Permitted Uses

You may use the Chalkberry name without separate permission when doing so accurately and non-misleadingly.

Examples include:

- referring to the official Chalkberry project;
- linking to the official Chalkberry repository;
- saying that your software is "based on Chalkberry";
- saying that your software is "derived from Chalkberry";
- stating that your software is "compatible with Chalkberry";
- discussing, reviewing, teaching, or writing about Chalkberry;
- identifying unmodified copies of the official Chalkberry software as Chalkberry.

Any such use must not suggest that your project, product, service, or organization is officially affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or maintained by the Chalkberry project unless that is actually the case.

## Forks and Modified Versions

You may fork, modify, redistribute, and commercially distribute Chalkberry in accordance with its software license.

However, if you distribute a modified version, you must not present that modified version in a way that is likely to cause users to believe that it is an official Chalkberry release.

A modified version must use a distinct product name and branding.

You may describe it truthfully using wording such as:

> Based on Chalkberry

or:

> Derived from the Chalkberry open-source project

You must not name a modified version in a way that implies official status, such as:

- Chalkberry Pro
- Chalkberry Plus
- Chalkberry Enterprise
- Chalkberry Premium
- Chalkberry Official
- Official Chalkberry

unless you have received explicit permission to do so.

## Redistribution of Unmodified Copies

You may redistribute exact, unmodified copies of official Chalkberry releases under the terms of the software license.

When doing so, you may identify the software as Chalkberry.

You must not falsely imply that:

- you created Chalkberry;
- you are the official Chalkberry publisher;
- your distribution is endorsed by João Geraldes;
- you are affiliated with the official Chalkberry project; or
- payment to you is required to obtain the official software.

If you charge money for redistributing an unmodified copy, you must make it clear that you are an independent distributor and not the official Chalkberry project.

## Commercial Use

Commercial use of the Chalkberry source code is governed by the software license.

Commercial use of the Chalkberry Marks must not create confusion about the source, sponsorship, endorsement, or official status of a product or service.

In particular, you may not use the Chalkberry Marks to market a third-party product or service as though it were an official Chalkberry product without permission.

## Logo Use

You may use the official Chalkberry logo when:

- referring to the official Chalkberry project;
- linking to the official Chalkberry project;
- identifying an unmodified official Chalkberry release; or
- using it in commentary, reviews, articles, or educational material where the context is clear.

You may not use the official Chalkberry logo as the primary branding of a modified version, fork, competing product, or unrelated service in a way that could cause confusion about its origin or endorsement.

Modified projects must use their own logo and visual identity.

## Domain Names, Package Names, and Store Listings

You must not use the Chalkberry name in a domain name, application-store publisher identity, package name, social-media account, organization name, or other identifier in a way that could reasonably be mistaken for an official Chalkberry property.

Names that clearly indicate an unofficial relationship may be acceptable where they are not misleading.

For example:

- `chalkberry-guide.example` may be acceptable for an independent informational site;
- `official-chalkberry.example` would not be acceptable without authorization.

## No Impersonation or False Endorsement

You may not use the Chalkberry Marks in a way that:

- falsely suggests sponsorship or endorsement;
